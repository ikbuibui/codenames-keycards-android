Create a fresh keycard whenever game night calls for one.

Codenames Keycards generates colorful spymaster keycards directly on your Android device. Set up a board that matches your table, then hand it to the spymaster.

FEATURES
• Generate keycards entirely offline.
• Choose board sizes from 2×2 to 10×10.
• Play with two, three, or four teams.
• Set the number of tiles for each team and choose the starting team.
• The starting team automatically receives one extra tile.
• Every keycard includes an assassin tile.
• Freeze a board to keep the exact same keycard when you close and reopen the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Codenames Keycards has no network permission, ads, analytics SDKs, web view, remote API, or downloaded game data. Your frozen board stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Codenames Keycards is an independent, unofficial companion app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Czech Games Edition.
